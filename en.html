This route invites you to discover the village of Montricoux and its artistic and architectural heritage. Through stories told by residents, artists, and cultural actors, let yourself be guided through the streets of this medieval village. Load the digital map by scanning the QR code at Station A (La Bascule) and move through the village to discover the audio capsules accessible with a single click.

Distance: approx. 2 km
Duration: approx. 30 min walk, 1 hour including listening points
